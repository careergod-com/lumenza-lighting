# ✦ LUMENZA Lighting

> **Brighter spaces. Better living.**

LUMENZA Lighting is a modern e-commerce concept focused on contemporary lighting, LED technology, smart illumination, decorative fixtures, architectural lighting, and solutions designed to transform the way people experience their spaces.

The project combines elegant visual design, practical product discovery, and a premium shopping experience for customers looking to illuminate homes, offices, commercial spaces, and outdoor environments.

---

## ✨ About LUMENZA

Light is more than functionality.

It can define the atmosphere of a room, highlight architectural details, create warmth, improve productivity, and completely change the character of a space.

LUMENZA was created around that idea.

The store brings together modern lighting products and smart illumination solutions in one carefully designed digital experience.

From a minimalist pendant lamp to architectural LED systems, LUMENZA is designed to help customers discover lighting that fits their space and their style.

---

## 💡 Our Concept

LUMENZA combines three ideas:

**LIGHT**

# 🖼️ Product Photography

Product photography is a fundamental part of the LUMENZA experience.

Every product image should be:

- Realistic
- Clean
- Professional
- Properly framed
- Centered
- Fully visible
- Consistent with the store layout
- Suitable for e-commerce
- Optimized for web performance

Product images should contain **only the product**.

They should never contain:

- Prices
- Buttons
- Product names
- Website interface
- Favorite icons
- Shopping-cart icons
- Promotional badges
- Ratings
- Random text
- Screenshots
- Unrelated products

All product information belongs to the website interface, not inside the product photograph.

Each product should have a specific image assigned to it.

# 🔎 Product Discovery

Lighting can be highly dependent on the space where it will be installed.

Future versions of LUMENZA can introduce an advanced **Lighting Finder**.

Customers could answer questions such as:

- What room are you lighting?
- Indoor or outdoor?
- What style do you prefer?
- How large is the space?
- Do you need adjustable brightness?
- Do you want smart controls?
- What color temperature do you prefer?
- Do you need decorative or functional lighting?

The system could then present relevant products.
